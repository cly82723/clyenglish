# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, multi-page, no build step. Chosen by the user.

## Users

Hong Kong secondary students in two tracks. Junior (S.1–S.3): vocab revision. Senior (S.4–S.6) preparing for HKDSE English Language Paper 2 (Writing): S.4 grammar, S.5 Part A, Part B and the Toolkit, S.6 final reminders. The S.5 Part B guide is pitched at intermediate-to-strong writers, on average around Level 4–5, working towards 5**; its comparisons run Level 3 → Level 5 → Level 5**. They read on phones between classes and on laptops when revising.

## Product Purpose

CLY English helps students raise their Paper 2 level by showing them, concretely, what each part of the paper asks for and how a stronger answer differs from a weaker one. Secondary aim: make English feel worth pursuing on their own, beyond the exam.

Success: a student can open the site, find the guide for the task they are practising in two taps, and leave knowing one specific thing to do differently in their next piece of writing.

## Positioning

A personal, single-voice guide (CLY) rather than a generic past-paper dump: original explanations organised by Part A / Part B and by text type, written to move students up a level.

## Operating Context

- HKDSE English Paper 2 (since the 2024 exam, 2 hours for both parts): Part A (compulsory short task, ~200 words) and Part B (choose one of four questions, no longer tied to the elective modules, ~400 words).
- Marking domains: Content, Language, Organisation.
- Students use the site alongside school practice papers and tutor feedback.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Core content: Part A and Part B guides. Part B is a step-by-step guide built on the three marking domains: Step 0 Choose & plan, Step 1 Content, Step 2 Organisation (CREI paragraphs for argumentative text types; own patterns for reports and stories), Step 3 Language, Step 4 Sample writings.
- Sources: any resource that helps students may be used: real past-paper tasks (cited by year and question), examiner reports, textbooks and dictionaries for tasks, ideas and further reading. Model writing on the site is written for the site. Level labels on samples are CLY's estimates, never official marks.
- Vocabulary is taught through context and collocation, with no Chinese translations.
- CLY English is a free resource (confirmed 2026-10-27). Do not add lesson sales, pricing, or booking flows.

## Product Principles

1. Every page should answer "what do I do differently to score higher?"
2. Serve every level: show the floor and the ceiling side by side, never only the top.
3. Two taps to any text-type guide.
4. Invite curiosity beyond the exam without diluting exam usefulness.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Students read English as a second language: plain sentence structure in explanations, legible type sizes on phones, WCAG AA contrast.
