# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, multi-page, no build step. Chosen by the user.

## Users

Hong Kong secondary students (mainly S4–S6) preparing for HKDSE English Language Paper 2 (Writing), across all ability bands: students sitting around Level 2–3 who need structure and confidence, and students at Level 4–5 reaching for 5*/5**. They read on phones between classes and on laptops when revising.

## Product Purpose

CLY English helps students raise their Paper 2 level by showing them, concretely, what each part of the paper asks for and how a stronger answer differs from a weaker one. Secondary aim: make English feel worth pursuing on their own, beyond the exam.

Success: a student can open the site, find the guide for the task they are practising in two taps, and leave knowing one specific thing to do differently in their next piece of writing.

## Positioning

A personal, single-voice guide (CLY) rather than a generic past-paper dump: original explanations organised by Part A / Part B and by text type, written to move students up a level.

## Operating Context

- HKDSE English Paper 2 (since the 2024 exam, 2 hours for both parts): Part A (compulsory short task, ~200 words) and Part B (choose one of four questions, no longer tied to the elective modules, ~400 words).
- Marking domains: Content, Language, Organisation.
- Students use the site alongside school practice papers and tutor feedback.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Core content: Part A and Part B guides, organised by text type, navigable via subtabs.
- Content is original teaching material written for the site; no copied HKEAA papers or sample scripts.
- Open: whether CLY is a tutor, centre, or free resource was not answered. Do not add lesson sales, pricing, or booking flows.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: CLY English.
- Logo: `images/image_1792159441498114.png` — a fox seated within the letters C-L-Y inside a copper ring. The site must use the image. Since 2026-10-24 the user chose a lively "picnic day" palette (sky blue, leaf green, mint, navy) over the logo's copper; the logo's fox orange stays as the one warm accent.
- Tone: professional, encouraging toward learners of every level.

## Evidence on Hand

- Logo image only.
- No testimonials, results statistics, student counts, contact details, or sample scripts exist. Do not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Every page should answer "what do I do differently to score higher?"
2. Serve every level: show the floor and the ceiling side by side, never only the top.
3. Two taps to any text-type guide.
4. Invite curiosity beyond the exam without diluting exam usefulness.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Students read English as a second language: plain sentence structure in explanations, legible type sizes on phones, WCAG AA contrast.
