Paper 2 cut-offs

The share of the marks needed for each level in Paper 2 (Writing), newest year first. Open a year to see the marks.

Estimates, not official. They come from tutoring centres and change every year, so use them as a rough guide only.

Where these figures come from

The HKEAA does not publish cut-off scores. These figures were worked out by tutoring centres, mostly from candidates' own result slips.

The main source is AfterSchool. Each year was checked against NoteSity and DSE Treasure. Where they disagree by more than a few points, the year's own note says which figure is shown.

The full mark changes from year to year, so compare years by percentage, not by marks. A mark with ≈ in front is approximate.
