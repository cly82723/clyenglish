What the marker reads for

Both parts are marked in three areas.

Content

Did you answer the question? Are your ideas developed and suited to the reader?

Quickest win: give every main point an example or a reason.

Language

Is your grammar accurate? Is your vocabulary precise? Do your sentences vary?

Quickest win: fix your three most common errors first.

Organisation

Does the writing flow? Are paragraphs clear? Does the format match the text type?

Quickest win: one idea per paragraph, with a clear opening sentence.

A two-hour time plan

One way to split the two hours. Try it in a mock paper, then adjust it to suit you.

Errors that cost Hong Kong students marks

Tick the ones you make. Check for those three every time you finish writing.

Instead ofWriteWhy
discuss about the issuediscuss the issue"Discuss" takes no preposition.
I very like reading.I really like reading."Very" can't describe a verb.
Although it rained, but we went.Although it rained, we went.Use "although" or "but", never both.
There have many people.There are many people.Translated from 有. English uses "there is / there are".
I was boring in class.I was bored in class.-ed is how you feel; -ing is what causes it.
a lot of informationsa lot of informationInformation, advice, equipment and homework have no plural.
The price is very expensive.The price is very high.Things are expensive; prices are high.
He suggested me to join.He suggested that I join."Suggest" doesn't take "someone to do".
It is depend on you.It depends on you."Depend" is a verb, not an adjective.
Every studentsEvery student"Every" is followed by a singular noun.
open the lightturn on the lightLights are turned on and off, not opened.
Students are lack of sleep.Students lack sleep. / Students suffer from a lack of sleep."Lack" as a verb has no "of".
