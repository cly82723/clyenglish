Tenses

The Tense Matrix

Every tense is a time plus an aspect. Choose a tense to see its formula and examples.

Quick quiz

Read the whole sentence before you choose: some time words point the wrong way. Wrong answers get a hint and another try. There are five sets.

Tense cloze

Pick a verb from the bank and type it in the right tense. Each verb is used once at most, and the tenses come in any order.

Three versions

3I have finished the paper yesterday.

5I finished the paper yesterday, so I had time to check my answers.

5**By the time the bell rang, I had already checked every answer twice.

CLY: Find the time words first (yesterday, since, by next year). They choose the tense for you.

The passive voice

Three versions

3The new library was build last year.

5The new library was built last year.

5**Built in just eighteen months, the new library has already become the district's favourite study space.

CLY: Use the passive when the doer doesn't matter: The results will be announced on Friday.

Gerunds & infinitives

Gerunds (V-ing as a noun)

To-infinitives (to + V1)

Three versions

3I'm interested in join the drama club.

5I'm interested in joining the drama club.

5**Joining the drama club taught me that speaking in front of people gets easier with practice.

CLY: After a preposition, use V-ing; after decide, hope or promise, use to + V1.

Conditionals

The Conditionals Matrix

Every conditional is how real plus when. Choose one to see its formula and a picture of it.

Quick quiz

Ask: is it real or imaginary, and when does it happen?

Match & change

Part A: match each sentence to its meaning. Part B: type the verbs in the right form.

Three versions

3If it will rain tomorrow, we stay home.

5If it rains tomorrow, we will stay home.

5**Had we checked the forecast, we would never have planned a picnic in a typhoon.

CLY: Never put will straight after if.
