The Tense Matrix

Every English tense combines a time (past, present, future) with an aspect (simple, continuous, perfect, perfect continuous). Tap any tense to see its formula, keywords and examples.

Tenses: short MC quiz

Choose the correct tense for each blank. Look for the keywords first. Each answer is marked as soon as you choose it, and if it's wrong, you'll get a hint and another try.

Tenses cloze: The Summer Intern

Fill in each blank by choosing a verb from the verb bank and typing it in the correct tense. Each verb is used once at most, and some verbs are not needed. All 12 blanks test a different tense. If an answer is wrong, you'll get a hint so you can fix it.

The Conditionals Matrix

Every conditional combines how real a situation is (real or imaginary) with when it happens (always, now and future, or past). Tap a conditional to see its formula, meaning, a picture of it and examples.

Conditionals: quick quiz

Choose the right verb form or the right meaning for each conditional. Ask yourself: is it real or imaginary, and when does it happen? Each answer is marked as soon as you choose it, and if it's wrong, you'll get a hint and another try.

Match & change: Rainstorms, Bubble Tea and Bad Luck

Part A: the sentences in each scene look alike, but they mean different things. Match each one to its meaning. Part B: read the situation, decide which conditional fits, and type the verbs in the right form.
