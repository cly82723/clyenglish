Practise this: Tenses

Watch one sentence climb to 5**

  1. 2

    I think phone is bad for student because they play it in class.

    The idea is there, but the grammar gets in the way.

  2. 3

    In my opinion, mobile phones are bad for students because they always play games in class.

    Accurate, but "bad" and "always" say nothing specific.

  3. 4

    Mobile phones distract students in class, as many find it hard to resist checking their messages.

    A precise verb, "distract", replaces "bad".

  4. 5

    Although phones can be useful learning tools, they often distract students, who find it hard to ignore a notification mid-lesson.

    It admits the other side first, then makes the point.

  5. 5**

    A phone in a pocket is a lesson half-heard: even when students ignore it, every buzz pulls a little attention away.

    One memorable image carries the whole argument.
