Summarise charts and tables.

Before you read

Which sentence summarises the 2022 chart better?

The second says what the numbers mean. The first only repeats two of them.

By the end you can describe a chart's trend in one sentence, and a table's pattern with a quantity word.

The 2022 Performance Report

Staff, profit and ranking in 1990 and 2022
19902022
Number of staff1465
Profit (HK$ millions)20214
Asia's top five lighting companiesShiny Diamond not in the top fiveShiny Diamond second

Say the shape, then the size

  • Staff: 14 to 65, so “more than quadrupled”.
  • Profit: 20 to 214 million, so “rose more than tenfold”.
  • Ranking: “climbed from outside the top five to second place”.
  • A list of figures with no verb: “14, then about 40, then 40, then 65”.

Use an exact number only when it is the point: a date, a price, a speed. The 2024 points ask for facts such as “65 knots” and “6 December”, so know which kind of number you are looking at.

A scale for percentages

A table row works the same way. In the 2022 customer survey, 93% chose “their warm glow” and 2% “old-fashioned”: “the vast majority love neon's warm glow, and hardly any call it old-fashioned”. Link the data to the task: the speech was about neon's lasting appeal, so the survey is evidence, not decoration.

Reading a calendar

The 2024 booking calendar shows which weeks are free. May weeks 1 and 2 are free, May weeks 3 and 4 are not, all of April is full, and all of June and July is free.

Try it

Profit went from HK$20 million in 1990 to HK$214 million in 2022. Which sentence is best?

Not quite. Have another look.

214 divided by 20 is about 10.7. “More than tenfold” gives the size without listing the numbers.

In 1990 the company was not in Asia's top five lighting companies. In 2022 it was second. Which sentence is best?

Not quite. Have another look.

The best sentence shows the change, which is the point of a comparison.

In the survey, 93% chose “their warm glow” and 2% “old-fashioned”. Which sentence is best?

Not quite. Have another look.

A quantity word reads naturally to a reader, and “hardly any” covers the 2%.

2024: you are telling the cancelled cruise's participants when they can rebook. Which sentence reads the calendar best?

Not quite. Have another look.

May weeks 3 and 4 are full, so the second and third sentences are wrong. This is points 9.7 and 9.8.
