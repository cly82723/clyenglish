Paper 3 · Part B2 · Skill 2 of 5

Read the Data File once, and tag it.

Before you read: Two students have five minutes with the Data File. Who prepares better?

Five minutes is not enough to read everything three times. One careful read with tags gives you the same information and leaves time to plan.

By the end you can turn the five minutes into a plan, and turn your notes into three outlines you are ready to write.

Before the recording: the five minutes

  1. 1. Task linesRead the three lines. Mark which ones use your notes (Skill 1).
  2. 2. Situation boxFind who you are, who your boss is, what you will hear and where the note sheet is. In 2024 you are Nico Lin, secretary to the manager, Ms Avery Goh, at World City Travel, and you will hear a Zoom meeting and note on page 3. Write down your name and job title.
  3. 3. The boss's emailThis is the instruction page. Underline every instruction and number it. In 2024: an internal incident report, starting from the handbook's guidelines; a follow-up email to the cancelled cruise's participants, starting with Shane Lee's email; a fun, exciting script for the Adventure Fishing Tour that explains how the new luxury yacht improves the customer experience, using the Sydney Times article too.
  4. 4. Tag each documentOne symbol per document: a circled 8, 9 or 10, or three highlighter colours. Some documents feed two tasks, so tag the part, not the page.
  5. 5. Read each document onceTick the instruction it answers. Strike out what no task asks for.
  6. 6. Note-sheet headingsThey tell you what the recording covers, and the sheet is shared with Part B1. In 2024: Guided Nature Walk, Bird Watching Tour, Sydney Whale Watching Cruise (the catamaran accident: the Review Board's findings, and compensation), Insects and Reptiles Exhibition, and Adventure Fishing Tour. The accident and the fishing tour are yours; strike out the rest.

After the recording

  1. 7. Match notes to pointsFor each task, list its points (step 3) and write beside each where it comes from: Data File, notes, or both. A point with no source means go back to your notes now, not while writing.
  2. 8. Plan each task in a minuteOpening, body points in order, closing. Use the text type's format: see Text types, and Part A: Formats for emails.
  3. 9. Settle the facts that disagreeCheck names, titles and dates against each other (below) before you write.

When two documents disagree

  • The news article (29 March) says the Adventure Fishing Tour runs from 15 November. The minutes (10 April) say the schedule now starts on 6 December. The later document, which announces the change, wins.
  • Shane Lee's email (2 April) names Chez Poisson as the caterer. The minutes (10 April) say iSea Caterers has taken over, with the same dishes.
  • Shane Lee's email wants the band Bubblegum on the yacht. The minutes say the live music was cancelled. Leave the band out.

Always ask which document is newest, and which one announces a change.

Check your points against the official list (2024)

Write your own list for each task first. This is HKEAA's Task Completion list, with the Data File pages the point comes from. A page with a star is information from the recording (page 3 of the Data File).

Task 8: incident report (8 points)

  1. 8.1The incident happened on 27 March at 5:50pm in Sydney Harbour. (3*, 5, 7)
  2. 8.2The catamaran crashed into rocks. (6)
  3. 8.3Reason: a sudden change in weather conditions. (6)
  4. 8.4No passengers were on board; the catamaran was training new crew members. (7)
  5. 8.5And scouting whale migration routes. (7)
  6. 8.6Review Board conclusion: Captain Moby was not at fault. (3*)
  7. 8.7He kept the crew on board safe and helped minimise damage to the catamaran. (10)
  8. 8.8He was extremely professional. (3*)

Task 9: follow-up email (9 points)

  1. 9.1We value our customers; we put customers first. (8)
  2. 9.2In addition to a full refund, we are offering a compensation package. (6, 7, 8)
  3. 9.3It includes lifetime membership of World City Travel. (3*)
  4. 9.4A 30% discount on all tours, for life. (3*)
  5. 9.5A free “Aqua Vita” photo print by Dr David Atkinson. (11)
  6. 9.6Participants can also choose another, complimentary, cruise. (10)
  7. 9.7For that cruise, the first two weeks of May are available. (8)
  8. 9.8June and July are available and include an additional 50% duration. (7, 8)
  9. 9.9Details of the available cruises are on the World City Travel website. (10)

Try it

Use the documents as in the 2024 Data File. Tap the best answer.

The Sydney Times article (29 March) gives the Adventure Fishing Tour's start as 15 November. The staff-meeting minutes (10 April) say it now starts on 6 December. Which date goes in the advertisement?

Not quite. Have another look.

The minutes are later and announce the change. This is point 10.1.

Shane Lee's email (2 April) says Chez Poisson will cater the buffet. The minutes (10 April) say something different. Which caterer do you name?

Not quite. Have another look.

The minutes announce that iSea Caterers took over, serving the same dishes (point 10.9).

Shane Lee's email wants live music by the band Bubblegum on the yacht. What do the minutes say?

Not quite. Have another look.

Dr Atkinson's concern that it would scare away the fish led to the cancellation. Mentioning the band would be a wrong fact.

The booking calendar (page 8) feeds mainly which task?

Not quite. Have another look.

It shows May weeks 1 and 2, and June and July, as available: points 9.7 and 9.8.

The company handbook extract (page 11) is mainly for…

Not quite. Have another look.

It is a second instruction page: date and time, location, what was involved, why people were there, the cause, and the Review Board's results.
