Paper 3 · Part B2 · Skill 3 of 5

Take notes while you listen.

Before you read: Which note will help you write fastest?

The speaker will not wait. Keep only what you cannot get from the Data File: numbers, names, reasons and opinions.

By the end you can note a speaker's facts, reasons and opinions in a few marks, tagged by task.

What to note

  • Under the printed headings, only (1) numbers, dates and names; (2) reasons and results; (3) what each speaker thinks.
  • Tag it: put the task number beside a note when you hear which task it serves (T8, T9, T10).
  • Strike out the headings you do not need, so your eye finds the right ones. In 2024 that was most of the sheet.
  • Skip anything already in the Data File, and do not copy whole sentences.

Practise on the real tapescript

These are lines from the 2024 recording, the part about the catamaran accident and the compensation. Read, or press Hear it, then choose the best note for each.

Avery: Well, speaking of which, we need to write the incident report. How's the Review Board's investigation on Captain Moby going? Have they reached a conclusion yet?

Shane: That's the good news. I've been sent the results of the Review Board's investigation and the conclusion is that Captain Moby is not at fault.

David: “Captain Moby not at fault”. He will be so relieved to hear that.

Shane: That's not all, David. The Review Board has also insisted that we explicitly mention in the incident report that Captain Moby was extremely professional in handling the situation.

Shane: So, I want to go the extra mile for this. As part of the additional compensation package, on top of what we've pledged already, we'll be giving participants of the cancelled cruise lifetime membership of World City Travel.

David: Lifetime membership of World City Travel? What does that include?

Avery: It's a great deal. With lifetime membership, participants can get a 30% discount on all tours that we provide. For life!

Avery: Right. So, during the Adventure Fishing Tour, the yacht will stop at two white sand beaches.

Shane: Two white sand beaches? Any particular reason for this choice? Why not on some sort of outlying islands?

Avery: That's because participants can catch shellfish at these stops. We chose these two beaches especially for that.

Try it: choose the best note

From the first passage: what the Review Board concluded.

Not quite. Have another look.

A few words, the fact, and the task tag. The long version is too slow; the short one loses the conclusion.

From the first passage: what the Board insisted on.

Not quite. Have another look.

The Board's demand and its exact words are what the report needs.

From the second passage: the compensation.

Not quite. Have another look.

Keep the number, the thing it applies to, and the tag. The first loses the 30%; the third is opinion, not a fact to write.

From the third passage: the stops on the fishing tour.

Not quite. Have another look.

The number, the place and the reason (∵) fit in a few marks.
