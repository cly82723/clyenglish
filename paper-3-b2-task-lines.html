Paper 3 · Part B2 · Skill 1 of 5

Read the three task lines first.

Before you read

Two students begin Part B2. Who protects the marks better?

The task line tells you what to use. In 2022, Task 9 named the Data File only. In 2024, all three tasks named the Data File and your notes. Check every year.

By the end you can say, for each task, the text type, the marks, the length, which sources it may use, and about how many content points it holds.

Marks tell you how many points to find

In the 2024 marking scheme, Task Completion is worth 8 marks in Task 8 (17 marks in all) and 9 marks in each of Tasks 9 and 10 (18 marks each). Each content point earns one mark, so those tasks hold 8, 9 and 9 points. The remaining marks go to Language, to Coherence and Organisation, and to Appropriateness.

A tutor's analysis of published exemplars (DSE Treasure) found the same pattern since 2017: 17 marks, about 8 points; 18 marks, about 9; 19 marks, about 10; 22 marks, about 13. Spend your time by marks, not by habit: the three tasks are close in weight, so none is a quick one.

Word counts: a guide, not a wall

The same analysis found that Level 5+ exemplars were on average about 1.8 times the stated length. A text that runs somewhat over is not an automatic failure, and cutting a needed point to stay near the number is the bigger risk. The sample is small (four published exemplars a year, three from one candidate), so treat it as a hint: cover every point first, keep each point short, and ask your teacher how your school marks length.

Try it

2022, Task 9: “…using information from the B2 Data File.” Which sources may you use?

Not quite. Have another look.

The line names only the Data File. Use the sources the line names, and nothing it does not.

2024, Task 9 is worth 18 marks. About how many content points should you find?

Not quite. Have another look.

Task Completion is 9 of the 18 marks, one per point.

A 2024 task says “around 180 words”. You have covered all nine points in 150 words. What now?

Not quite. Have another look.

Points first, then quality. Filler adds nothing, and length is not ignored, so ask your teacher how your school marks it.
