Part B2 first: three texts built from a Data File and your listening notes. Five skills, one page each.

Part B2 · Integrated Skills

You get five minutes to read the Question-Answer Book and the Data File, then you listen and make notes on page 3 of the Data File, then you write all three texts. The Data File is not collected, and it is not for writing your answers in. [HKEAA 2022 instructions]
