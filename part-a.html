Part A: the short task

Four skills, from reading the task to the opening paragraph. Each is one short page.

1 Read the taskUse every point in the task Done Weaker: Drop books before 13 March. Stronger: Drop your books at Room 204 by 13 March, and each one earns you a swap token for the fair. 2 FormatsGet the format right Done Weaker: Dear all students, … Yours faithfully, Chris Stronger: Dear fellow students, … Best regards, Chris Wong, Chairperson, English Club 3 PlanPlan in five minutes Done Weaker: Starts writing at once and tries to remember the notes. Stronger: Numbers the notes, groups them, and plans one extra detail for each paragraph. 4 OpeningOpen so both purposes show Done Weaker: I am Chris Wong. I am writing to tell you about the book fair. It will be held on 15 March in the school hall. Please come. Stronger: Every bookshelf has one: the novel you finished in Form 2 and never opened again.
