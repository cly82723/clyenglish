Where these questions come from

HKDSE questions are reproduced by permission of the Hong Kong Examinations and Assessment Authority (HKEAA), which owns the papers and sells them through its publications page. Always practise from the real paper when you can.

The wording of each year's questions comes from the source named under that year. Where a question includes a picture, a poster or song lyrics, the page says so instead of showing them. The 2026 questions are still CLY's summaries. A blue text type links to CLY's guide for it. Up to 2023, each Part B question belonged to one of eight elective modules, shown beside the question; from 2024 there are four Part B questions and no electives.

The sample answers in the 2024 and 2025 cards quote real candidates from the HKEAA's official exemplars, marked by examiners (from the HKEAA website). They are typed from the candidates' handwriting with their mistakes kept, because the mistakes are part of the level. The official exemplars stop at Level 5, so each 5** version is CLY's own. The older cards quote tutors' published samples, so treat their levels as the tutors' claims. The analysis is CLY's.

Which topics come back

Each question that fits one of the Toolkit's seven topics carries a small tag, and the tag links to that topic's words. The groupings are CLY's own, not the HKEAA's. Stories, diaries and other creative tasks are left untagged, and a question can fit two topics.

  • Education & school life 18 questions, in 10 of 14 years, last in 2026
  • Technology & social media 7 questions, in 7 of 14 years, last in 2026
  • Environment 3 questions, in 3 of 14 years, last in 2023
  • Health & well-being 7 questions, in 7 of 14 years, last in 2025
  • Youth & social issues 10 questions, in 7 of 14 years, last in 2026
  • Hong Kong culture & tourism 19 questions, in 10 of 14 years, last in 2024
  • Work & careers 16 questions, in 12 of 14 years, last in 2026

73 of the 114 questions on this page carry a tag. No topic appears in every year, so the safest preparation is to know the words for all seven.
