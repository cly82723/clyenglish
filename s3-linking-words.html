Unit 4

Linking words

although, because, in case

Before you read

Which sentence is correct?

Use one contrast word. "Although" already links the two parts.

One check: For every linking word, check two things: does its job match the ideas (add, contrast, cause …)? And does the right grammar follow it (a noun or a clause, a new sentence or the same one)?
