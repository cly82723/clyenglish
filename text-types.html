Text types

Six text types, one page each. Pick the one your question asks for.

1 ArticleAn article is written for readers of a magazine, newsletter or website. Done 2 Letter to the editorYou're writing to a newspaper to argue a point in public. The editor is your addressee, but your real audience is every reader of that newspaper. Done 3 SpeechA speech is writing for the ear. If it doesn't sound good read aloud, it isn't finished. Done 4 ProposalA proposal asks someone with power, such as a principal or a committee, to approve a plan. It wins by being practical, not passionate. Done 5 ReportA report tells a reader what was found and what should be done. Facts first, opinions last, and clearly labelled. Done 6 StoryA story is judged on how well you control the reader's experience. Show what happened; don't just report it. Done
