Before you read: Which sentence says more?

The second says what sleep is important for. This page gives you swaps for good, bad, very important and more.

By the end you can swap a vague word for a word that says exactly what you mean.

Choose the words that say exactly what you mean, not the longest ones.

OverusedTryIn a sentence
goodrewarding, valuable, beneficialVolunteering is a rewarding way to spend a weekend.
badharmful, damaging, counterproductiveCramming the night before can be counterproductive.
very importantessential, crucial, vitalSleep is essential for memory.
a lot ofa growing number of, countless, a large proportion ofA growing number of teenagers work part-time.
helpenable, allow, encourageThe scheme would enable students to borrow tablets.
problemissue, challenge, obstacleCost is the main obstacle.
thinkbelieve, argue, am convincedI am convinced that the levy works.
bigsignificant, considerable, majorThe change made a significant difference.
  • Learn a new word together with the words it usually goes with: make a difference, pose a challenge, raise awareness.
