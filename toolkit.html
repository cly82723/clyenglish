The language toolkit

Four skills and a word bank. Each skill is one short page.

Writing Part B? The Part B guide takes you step by step through Content, Organisation and Language.

1 ToolkitOpenings that make a marker look up Done Weaker: Nowadays, many students use phones in class. Stronger: When did you last sit through a lesson without checking your phone? 2 ToolkitLinking ideas Done Weaker: Firstly, students are tired. Secondly, they cannot focus. Stronger: Many students are tired; as a result, they cannot focus in class. 3 ToolkitPrecise vocabulary Done Weaker: Sleep is very important for students. Stronger: Sleep is essential for memory. 4 ToolkitSentence variety Done Weaker: The school has a new rule. Students cannot use phones. Many students are unhappy about it. Stronger: Since the school banned phones last month, many students have been unhappy, arguing that they need them to check homework updates. 5 ToolkitWords by topic Done Weaker: give students skills Stronger: equip students with transferable skills
